# Flash AI - User Guide

## Welcome to Flash AI! ⚡

Your intelligent AI assistant is ready to help you with questions, creative writing, coding, and more.

## Getting Started

### 1. Create an Account

1. Visit the login page
2. Click on the **"Sign Up"** tab
3. Choose a username (letters, numbers, and underscores only)
4. Create a password (at least 6 characters)
5. Click **"Create Account"**

Your email will be automatically generated as `{username}@miaoda.com`

### 2. Sign In

1. Visit the login page
2. Enter your username
3. Enter your password
4. Click **"Sign In"**

## Features

### 💬 Chat with AI

- Type your question or message in the input box
- Press **Enter** or click the **Send** button
- Get instant AI-powered responses
- Continue the conversation naturally

### 📝 Conversation History

- All your conversations are automatically saved
- Access previous conversations from the sidebar
- Click on any conversation to continue where you left off
- Your chat history persists even after closing the browser

### 🎨 Beautiful Interface

- Modern, clean design
- Smooth animations and transitions
- Dark mode support (toggle in the header)
- Responsive layout for all devices

### 🔒 Persistent Login

**Your login session stays active!**

- Once you log in, you stay logged in
- Close the browser and come back - you're still logged in
- No need to log in every time
- Your session is securely stored

To log out:
1. Click your avatar in the top right
2. Select **"Sign Out"**

### 📤 Export Conversations

- Click the **"Export"** button in the header
- Download your conversation as a text file
- Keep a backup of important chats

### 🗑️ Clear Conversations

- Click the **"Clear"** button to start fresh
- This will clear the current conversation
- Previous conversations remain in history

### ⭐ Upgrade Your Plan

- Free plan includes basic features
- Upgrade to unlock:
  - Higher upload limits
  - Priority support
  - Advanced features
- Click **"Upgrade"** in the header to view plans

## Tips for Best Results

### Ask Clear Questions

✅ Good: "Can you explain how photosynthesis works in simple terms?"
❌ Less clear: "plants?"

### Provide Context

✅ Good: "I'm learning React. Can you explain useState hook with an example?"
❌ Less clear: "explain useState"

### Break Down Complex Topics

Instead of asking one huge question, break it into smaller parts:
1. "What is machine learning?"
2. "What are the main types of machine learning?"
3. "Can you give me an example of supervised learning?"

## Troubleshooting

### Can't Log In?

1. Check your username and password
2. Username should only contain letters, numbers, and underscores
3. Password must be at least 6 characters
4. Try refreshing the page

### Messages Not Saving?

1. Make sure you're logged in
2. Check your internet connection
3. Try refreshing the page
4. Check browser console for errors (F12)

### Session Not Persisting?

1. Make sure cookies are enabled in your browser
2. Check that localStorage is not disabled
3. Try clearing browser cache and logging in again

### UI Issues?

1. Try refreshing the page
2. Clear browser cache
3. Try a different browser
4. Make sure JavaScript is enabled

## FAQ

**Q: How long does my session last?**
A: Your session persists indefinitely until you log out or clear browser data.

**Q: Can I use Flash AI on mobile?**
A: Yes! The interface is fully responsive and works on all devices.

**Q: Is my data secure?**
A: Yes, we use industry-standard encryption and security practices.

**Q: Can I delete my conversations?**
A: Yes, use the "Clear" button to remove conversations.

**Q: What's the difference between plans?**
A: Higher plans offer increased upload limits and additional features.

**Q: Can I change my username?**
A: Currently, usernames cannot be changed after account creation.

**Q: How do I enable dark mode?**
A: Click the theme toggle button in the header (sun/moon icon).

**Q: Can I export all my conversations at once?**
A: Currently, you can export one conversation at a time.
