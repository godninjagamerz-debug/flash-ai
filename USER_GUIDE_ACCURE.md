# Flash AI & Accure AI - User Guide

## Welcome to Dual AI Experience! 🚀

Flash AI now offers two powerful AI assistants to choose from, each with its own unique personality and visual theme.

---

## 🌟 Meet Your AI Assistants

### ⚡ Flash AI (Default)
- **Best For**: Quick responses, general questions, everyday tasks
- **Speed**: Ultra-fast (5-50ms depending on plan)
- **Theme**: Professional blue gradient
- **Icon**: Lightning bolt
- **Availability**: All plans (Free, Pro, Business, Hero)

### ✨ Accure AI (Premium)
- **Best For**: Precise answers, complex tasks, detailed analysis
- **Speed**: Fast (15ms for Business/Hero)
- **Theme**: Warm yellow gradient
- **Icon**: Sparkles
- **Availability**: Pro, Business, and Hero plans only

---

## 🎯 How to Switch Between AI Models

### On Desktop (Large Screens)
**For Pro/Business/Hero Users:**
1. Look at the top-right corner of the screen
2. You'll see two buttons: "Flash AI" and "Accure"
3. Click the button for the AI you want to use
4. The entire interface will smoothly transition to match your choice

### On Mobile & Tablets
**For All Users:**
1. Look at the top-right corner of the screen
2. You'll see a dropdown button showing your current AI
3. Tap the dropdown to see both options:
   - ⚡ Flash AI
   - ✨ Accure (Pro Only) ← if you're on Free plan
4. Select your preferred AI
5. The interface will update automatically

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: Can I use Accure on the Free plan?
**A:** No, Accure AI is a premium feature available for Pro, Business, and Hero plans. However, you can see it in the dropdown to know what you're missing!

### Q: What happens if I try to use Accure without a Pro plan?
**A:** You'll see a friendly notification: "Accure AI is only available for Pro and Business plans. Upgrade to access this feature." Your selection will stay on Flash AI.

### Q: Can I switch between AIs during a conversation?
**A:** Yes! You can switch at any time. Your conversation history is preserved.

### Q: Will my messages look different with each AI?
**A:** The AI responses may differ in style and detail, but the message format stays the same.

### Q: Does Accure cost extra beyond my plan?
**A:** No! Once you have a Pro, Business, or Hero plan, Accure is included at no extra cost.

### Q: Which AI is better?
**A:** Neither is "better" - they're different! Flash is faster and great for general use. Accure is more precise and detailed. Choose based on your needs.

### Q: Can I set a default AI?
**A:** Currently, Flash AI is the default for all users. You can switch to Accure anytime during your session.

### Q: Does the AI choice affect image/video generation?
**A:** No, image and video generation use specialized models regardless of your AI selection.
